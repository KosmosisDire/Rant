DART bridge — video over WebRTC

Two nodes in this page: a camera that encodes an animated test pattern with WebCodecs (H264) and publishes it inside a CamSample { pose: Pose, frame: VideoFrame } message (and latches a JPEG of it in an Image variable, snapshot), and a viewer that subscribes to the samples (reading the pose in its handler) and attaches a VideoView to the frame field. Over WebRTC the bridge carries that field on a real video track (the browser decodes it, the frames arrive with the pixels emptied); add ?transport=websocket to watch the view decode the frames itself. ?topic=NAME views anything on the mesh by name instead: the page asks the mesh what NAME is (a topic or a variable), opens it typed by reflection, and attaches the view to the first VideoFrame, Image or ExternalVideoStream in its type (try ?topic=snapshot). Open with dart_bridge running (?bridge=ws://host:7480 for a remote one).
